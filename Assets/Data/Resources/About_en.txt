Thank you for walking with Cus across the slope, the sky, and that star-strewn universe.

<i>Neither ending is right or wrong. In one, he turns and walks away; in the other, he lies down to look at the stars. Both are true, both are him — and maybe, <color=#0047AB>both are us.</color></i>

This story began with a dream I had years ago. When I woke up, a feeling I couldn't put into words, stuck in my throat, wrapped itself around me, and I was determined to write it down. After the rapids of time had washed over it, only broken fragments were left. Still, I picked them up and pieced them together into this story.

I'm a student, and I wanted to make something over the National Day holiday. I happened to see how remarkably capable the Claude Opus 5.5 model was, and my wish to make a visual novel took root. <color=#0047AB>One person and one model: what could they make in seven days?</color> I know my skills fall far short of what making a game takes — writing, drawing, programming, music, there's hardly anything I could show off. But AI helped me so, so much with all of these, and let me make visible the feelings and emotions I wanted to express and to capture.

And thank you, too, for patiently hearing out such a childish dream and playing through this humble little work. If it stirred even the faintest echo in you, that would be a great honour for me.
